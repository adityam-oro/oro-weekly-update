May Summary

  • On a per-working-day basis ND at 84.7/day vs 80.4/day in April -- 6 of 8 cities improved their daily run rate. The raw MoM +0.62% understates the improvement given May had one fewer working day.
  • AUM crossed ₹1,200 Cr and is up 35.9% since January. Net addition of ₹41.99 Cr is ahead of April despite releases rising -- Vijayawada remains the only city with a negative net addition at -₹1.7 Cr.
  • Renewal book recovering at +31.2% MoM (1,329 vs 1,013 in April). Release value at ₹147.07 Cr is up from April but growing slower than renewals this month.
  • Bengaluru had the strongest recovery among T1 cities -- +9.4% ND volume MoM and +11.6% in value. Hyderabad remains the anchor city at 33.4% of total ND volume.
  • New customer acquisition declined 5.4% MoM to 884 -- Chennai (-21.6%), Warangal (-32%), Vijayawada (-13.3%) are declining. Hyderabad and Pune held steady.
  • Warangal is the only city running below its April per-day rate on both volume (-11.8%) and value (-31.5%). Karimnagar is a positive -- ND value up 54.5% MoM despite modest volume growth.
