Key Highlights
✓ What Worked Well
  • Daily run rate improved: 95.1 loans/day (17 WDs) vs 85.5/day in May (21 WDs) — strongest daily rate in three months.
  • Guntur standout recovery: +28.6% volume, +90.2% value MoM — best MoM growth of any city in June.
  • Warangal stabilised: +17.2% volume recovery after three consecutive months of decline.
  • Pune resilience: Lowest volume decline at -6.1% MoM; best DV achievement at 200% of revised target.
  • Vijayawada new customers grew: 32 new customers vs 27 in May (+18.5%) — first reversal in the trend.
  • Audit quality at highest level: Process audit accuracy hit 96.75% — up 2.56% from May; Kit audit at 95.31%.
  • DV target nearly met: 217 of 256 revised target achieved (85%) — conversion from visit to loan remains solid.
△ Needs Improvement
  • ND value declined sharply: ₹74.6 Cr vs ₹98.6 Cr in May (-24.3%). Average loan value dropped to ₹4.6L — lowest since February.
  • Net addition fell 54%: ₹15.5 Cr MTD vs ₹40.1 Cr in May. Three cities (Bengaluru, Vijayawada, Guntur) posted negative net addition.
  • Declining WoW trend: W26 closed at just 282 loans / ₹11.8 Cr — the weakest week of the month.
  • New customer acquisition continues to fall: 780 MTD vs 865 in May (-9.8%) — fifth consecutive month of decline from the Feb peak of 1,451.
  • Karimnagar sharp reversal: -36.4% volume, -66.8% value after the strong May performance — needs investigation.
  • A2NU conversion low across the board: Outbound A2NU at 6.8% vs 17.7% target. Digital A2NU at 10% vs 33% target.
  • Hiring below target: 55% achievement (72 of 131). Chennai and Hyderabad remain the critical gaps.

✓ What Worked Well — Support
  • Live pickup improved in final week: Jun 22–27 achieved 52.7% live pickup vs 42.8% in Jun 14–21 — a 9.9pp improvement.
  • Eventually spoke rate consistently high: 86.7–89.5% across all periods — callbacks are working effectively.

△ Needs Improvement — Support
  • Overall resolution below target: 50.5% MTD resolution rate. Nearly half of tickets remain unresolved at close.
  • “Others” category is a black hole: 277 tickets, only 9.4% resolved, 71.5% unreachable — needs categorisation and escalation SOP.
