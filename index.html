June Summary

  • ND volume down slightly, -1.5% MoM to 1,767. Value down 19.3% to ₹79.6 Cr - volume held, ticket size shrank.
  • New customers nearly flat at 850, -1.6% MoM - not a decline, a plateau.
  • Fresh loans grew +10.6% MoM (loan-type table); Takeover fell -17.8% and is the real drag on value.
  • Hyderabad (+4.6%) and Pune (+2.7%) grew ND volume this month. Warangal (+31.0%) and Guntur (+42.9%) grew too. Chennai, Bengaluru, Vijayawada, Karimnagar declined.
  • AUM growth slowed to +1.33% (₹16.3 Cr net addition), down from +3.40% in May.
  • Gold rate fell 8.3% within June (₹14,125 on Jun 5 to ₹12,807 by Jun 26) - likely why value fell harder than volume across most cities.
