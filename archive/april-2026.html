Key Highlights
✓ What Worked Well
  • Second half recovery: Apr 13-28 delivered Rs 51.4 Cr ND vs Rs 27.4 Cr in the first half -- nearly double.
  • Takeover recovered from Rs 1.2 Cr (Wk 14) to Rs 11-12 Cr in Wks 15-17. Bouncing back, though still a long way to go on target.
  • AUM crossed Rs 1,182 Cr with consistent week-on-week growth across all 4 weeks.
  • Hyderabad: although appointment count was lower than target, DVs closed at 85% -- strong conversion from visit to loan.
  • Chennai DV conversion at 72.3% vs 70.6% target -- the only city to beat the DV conversion target.
  • Week 16 was our best week: Rs 30.2 Cr ND, 481 loans, 1.70% AUM growth.
△ Needs Improvement
  • Monthly ND at 52%: Rs 94.6 Cr vs Rs 181.1 Cr target. External factors -- gold rate movements, bank holidays -- hurt the start of the month.
  • Net addition at 36%: Rs 44.5 Cr vs Rs 124.6 Cr target. Lower Takeover volumes and renewals being suppressed in the first week were the main drivers.
  • New cities remain fragile. Working on leveraging digital and locally sourced leads across Vijayawada, Warangal, and Guntur.
  • Bengaluru needs to improve conversions across the board. Sales team to be more proactive in meeting customers and raising visits.
  • Guntur had a really tough month due to people constraints -- urgent fix required.
